<section class="section intro-section" id="research">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">RESEARCH PROGRAM</p>
    <h2>Reliable Resource-Efficient AI</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="research-intro">
    <p class="large-copy">
      My central question is simple:
      <strong>How can we build AI systems that remain reliable when data, computation,
      connectivity and sensing quality are limited?</strong>
    </p>
    <p>
      This research brings together efficient learning, robust signal processing,
      trustworthy machine learning, model compression and edge deployment.
      Healthcare is the first proving ground, with applications extending to energy,
      agriculture and other resource-constrained settings.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-grid">
    <article class="research-card">
      <span class="card-number">01</span>
      <h3>Data-efficient learning</h3>
      <p>Learning from small, noisy, incomplete and imbalanced datasets.</p>
      <div class="tags"><span>Robust ML</span><span>SSL</span><span>Noise</span></div>
    </article>
    <article class="research-card">
      <span class="card-number">02</span>
      <h3>Efficient architectures</h3>
      <p>Models designed around accuracy, memory, latency and energy constraints.</p>
      <div class="tags"><span>CNN</span><span>Transformers</span><span>Edge AI</span></div>
    </article>
    <article class="research-card">
      <span class="card-number">03</span>
      <h3>Trustworthy AI</h3>
      <p>Rules, uncertainty, calibration and domain knowledge for safer predictions.</p>
      <div class="tags"><span>Explainability</span><span>Rules</span><span>Uncertainty</span></div>
    </article>
    <article class="research-card">
      <span class="card-number">04</span>
      <h3>Compression & deployment</h3>
      <p>Pruning, quantization and distillation for practical on-device intelligence.</p>
      <div class="tags"><span>Quantization</span><span>Pruning</span><span>TinyML</span></div>
    </article>
  </div>

  <div class="research-principle">
    <span>Research principle</span>
    <strong>Build. Measure. Reproduce. Deploy.</strong>
  </div>
</section>
